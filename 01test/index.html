Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship

Digital Citizenship is the responsible, safe, and ethical use of digital technologies, such as the internet, social media, computers, and mobile devices.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law refers to the rules, regulations, and legal rights that govern the use of technology, computers, digital devices, and the internet.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use an organization's technology resources, such as computers, networks, internet access, email, and devices.

What is Plagerism?

Plagerism is the act of using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.

It doesn't matter whether it's done on purpose or by accident, it is still plagerism.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic Dishonesty is any form of cheating or dishonest behavior related to schoolwork, exams, research, or other academic activities.

It is cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork.

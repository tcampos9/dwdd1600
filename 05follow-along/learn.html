Learning About Keto

The ketogenic diet focuses on reducing carbohydrates while increasing fats. Learning about the foods commonly included in a keto diet can help you better understand how the diet works.

Planning Your Meals

Planning meals ahead of time can make it easier to choose foods that fit into a ketogenic diet. Foods such as eggs, cheese, fish, meat, and certain vegetables are commonly included.

Do Your Research

Before making major changes to your diet, it is important to learn more about nutrition and understand how different foods affect your body.
